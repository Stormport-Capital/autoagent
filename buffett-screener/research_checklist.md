# Buffett candidate research checklist (the human C4 layer)

The screener automates only the quantitative proxy layer. It cannot see the
things that actually make Buffett's method work. Run this on **every** name the
screen surfaces, in order. A **NO** on any GATE kills the candidate no matter
how clean the numbers were — the numbers are only proxies for gates 2–3.

---

### GATE 1 — Circle of competence
- Can you explain in plain language how this business makes money, and how it
  will still make money in 10 years?
- If no → **STOP.** Not a candidate for you, regardless of the metrics.

### GATE 2 — Moat durability (what the screen only *proxies* via margins/ROIC)
- What specifically prevents a well-funded competitor from taking these returns?
  (brand, switching costs, network effects, low-cost scale, patents/regulation)
- Is the moat **widening or narrowing**? Cite evidence, not story.
- Counter-example to calibrate against: **Dexter Shoes** passed the numbers and
  still lost its moat to imports → Buffett's worst deal. Clean metrics are not a moat.

### GATE 3 — One-time problem vs terminal decline (the value-trap killer)
- If the stock is cheap, WHY? Write the bear case in one sentence.
- Is the problem **temporary and fixable** (Buffett's American Express salad-oil,
  GEICO) or **terminal** (Berkshire's own textile mill — the archetype)?
- Would you be comfortable if the market closed for 5 years after you bought?

### GATE 4 — Management quality & capital allocation
- Candid with owners? Read the last 3 shareholder letters — do they admit mistakes?
- Where do retained earnings go, and does each retained dollar create ≥ $1 of value?
- Any dilution, empire-building M&A, or aggressive accounting?

### GATE 5 — Valuation & margin of safety
- Estimate intrinsic value yourself (Buffett never publishes his — this is C4).
- Earnings yield vs the current 10y Treasury: are you paid to own the equity?
- Is there a **margin of safety** to your estimate? (Note: "margin of safety"
  means earnings-power discount here — NOT Klarman's asset-value sense. See
  `conflicts_open.md` / CONFLICT-001. Do not merge the two.)
- P/E near 40 vs the name's own history → this is an EXIT flag, not an entry.

---

### If all five gates pass
- Size per your position-sizing rule (operator parameters U-11 / U-12).
- Record the thesis and the specific bear case you rejected.
- Monitor via the sell triggers: moat erosion, P/E ≥ 40 vs own history, target met.
  Otherwise hold — there is no time stop on a genuine compounder.
