Daily routine (in this order)

Pre-market (before 9:30 ET)

  1. Open the dashboard (Tailscale on) and check the top line: Data: fmp, a recent Last check, and Backed up with today's or yesterday's date.
  2. For each new idea: type the symbol in Add symbols, pick Short only or Long & short, pick the Trade grade, tick the books you want under Add to, press Add to watchlist.
  3. Names you're done with: Remove (closes anything open first). Names to keep but not re-enter: Pause.
  4. Click each book tab once and check the Alpaca card says sync on and "paper matches the model".

At the open and during the day

  1. Nothing to press: the bot checks on its own (see when).
  2. Glance at the Watchlist for open positions and the Alpaca card for a red "N symbol(s) differ" line.
  3. A name that's chopping you up: Pause it (and Flatten if you want out now).

Before the close (3:45–3:55 PM ET)

  1. Review Open positions on each tab. Anything you don't want to hold overnight: Flatten, then Pause. Do this before 4:00 (see why).

After the close

  1. Open Trade Review, click today on the calendar, skim the trades, tag anything notable and write a one-line day note (how).
  2. Optional: export to TradesViz (how).
  3. The backup runs by itself after 4:10 PM.

Weekly

  1. Clean the watchlist: remove names that no longer fit.
  2. Trade Review → 7D or 30D: read What the numbers say, then the Breakdown tabs Strategy, Book, Symbol, Time of day and Exit type (the 30-minute review).
  3. Optional: Download backup on each tab to keep a copy on your PC.

How to…

Add a symbol to only one book

  1. Type it, set direction and grade.
  2. Untick the two books you don't want under Add to.
  3. Add to watchlist.

Get out of a position now

  1. Go to the book tab that holds it.
  2. Flatten on the symbol's row, confirm.
  3. Pause (or Remove) so it doesn't re-enter.
  4. Repeat on every book tab that holds the stock.

A ticker changed (e.g. AIXC → FFR)

  1. Go to a book tab that holds the old ticker.
  2. Rename on its row → type the new ticker.
  3. Split ratio: 1 for a plain name change; for a 1-for-10 reverse split type 10 (shares ÷ 10, prices × 10).
  4. Repeat on every book tab that holds it.
  5. Open the Alpaca paper app for that book. If it still shows the old ticker, the bot sends nothing for the new one and logs broker-wait. Once Alpaca shows the new ticker it carries on; or close the old ticker in the Alpaca app and the bot re-opens it under the new one.

Day-trade a name (out by the close, hands-off)

  1. On each book tab that holds it, click EOD close: off so it reads on.
  2. Leave it. It keeps trading long and short; at 3:55 PM ET it closes everything at that 5-minute close and takes no new entries until the next morning.
  3. Click again to turn it off and allow overnight holds.

If the server was down at 3:55, the model still books the exit at that day's 3:55 price when it catches up, but the paper order only goes out when the server is back.

Stop a choppy name from trading

Pause it in the books where it chops (often 5-min and 15-min) and leave it active in the hourly book. Paused names still exit by the rules.

Turn paper orders on or off for a book

Book tab → Alpaca card → tick or untick Send the model's trades to Alpaca paper. Turning it on opens any positions the model already holds.

Check a signal against your chart

Bars on the row. Highlighted rows fired a signal; the VWAP column is the value the engine used.

Export to TradesViz

  1. Book tab → bottom tabs → Closed trades.
  2. Pick from / to dates (trades are picked by the day they were opened). Tick "include open positions" only if you want entries that haven't closed.
  3. Download CSV. Two rows per trade: Open and Close.
  4. In TradesViz, use the custom CSV import: map Date, Time, Symbol, Action, Quantity, Price, Fees; timezone US/Eastern. Export date ranges that don't overlap to avoid duplicates.

Change the portfolio size or a rule

Book tab → Portfolio settings → change → Save settings. Only that book changes.

See why a trade happened

Bottom tab Signal log: every entry, exit and skip with the bar and the numbers. Closed trades shows each exit's reason.

Things to know

  • Tags are yours. Use them for what the bot can't know: catalyst (offering, earnings, PR), your view (chased, would have held, bad fill). Then filter by Tag or use the Tag breakdown.
  • Grade is the grade the symbol had when the trade opened. Trades opened before this page existed show the symbol's current grade, marked with *.
  • Sector, market cap and float come from FMP (needs FMP_API_KEY) and are today's values, not the trade date's; diluting small caps change fast. The first visit looks them up in the background; refresh after a minute. "Unknown" means FMP had nothing (often a renamed or delisted ticker).
  • A VWAP scale-out is two rows: the half covered at the 10-day MA and the rest.
  • Remove keeps a symbol's trades here; only Reset portfolio deletes them.

A 30-minute weekly review

  1. Set 7D. Read the tiles and What the numbers say.
  2. Breakdown → Symbol: is one name doing most of the damage? Pause it in the books where it chops.
  3. Breakdown → Strategy and Book: which tranche and timeframe are paying?
  4. Breakdown → Time of day and Exit type: when do entries work, and do your manual exits help or hurt?
  5. Filter by your tags (e.g. chased) and note what they cost. Write the week's takeaway as a day note.

When it checks (about 2 minutes after each bar closes, ET): Hourly at 10:00 … 15:00; 15-min at 9:45 … 15:45; 5-min at 9:35 … 15:55. Each book also checks at the 9:30 open for the previous day's last bar. A symbol only trades on signals after you add it.

Size: equity × grade % ÷ 3 per tranche, divided by the risk per share; total exposure capped at 2× equity.

Reading the numbers

  • S 522 −$387 in a tranche column: short 522 shares, open P&L −$387. L = long. flat = no position.
  • Green = profit, red = loss. A red VWAP value on the watchlist means price is below VWAP.
  • R = profit or loss measured in units of the risk taken. −1R = lost the planned risk.
  • Alpaca card "N symbol(s) differ from the model": the paper account doesn't hold what the model holds, usually a short Alpaca rejected (hard to borrow) or an order still working. Check Paper orders.
  • Model prices are the bot's simulated fills. Alpaca paper fills can differ, especially on fast stops.

Don'ts

Don't close positions in the Alpaca app. The model still holds them and the next check reopens them. Always exit with Flatten.
Don't Flatten after 4:00 PM ET. The model exits at the last price, but the paper order waits for the next morning's open, so the two disagree.
Don't run the PC copy (Start Dashboard.bat) while the server runs. Both would trade the same paper accounts.
Lower timeframes chop. A single sideways name can rack up many small losses on the 5- and 15-min books (TJGC was 90% of the 15-min losses). Pause choppy names there, or add them to the hourly book only.
